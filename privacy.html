Privacy Policy (English)

Rift Dominion · Effective November 12, 2026

Rift Dominion does not collect any personal data. The app has no accounts, servers, advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Data on your device. Scores, progress, achievements, settings and practice bookmarks are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to the developer or third parties. Deleting the app removes them. Backup files you choose to export are stored where you choose and remain under your control.

In-app purchases. Since version 1.3.0 the app is completely free, has no in-app purchases and does not check purchases with the App Store. Purchases made in earlier versions were processed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; the developer never receives your name, payment details or Apple Account.

Third parties. No data is shared with third parties, and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children. The app does not collect data from children.

Changes. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
